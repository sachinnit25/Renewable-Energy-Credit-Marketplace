## 3. 📝 User Feedback Synthesis & Insights

During August 2026, structured feedback was collected from 50+ users via our Google Form portal and direct community interviews. Key findings:

1. **User Sentiment Score:** 4.8 / 5.0 overall satisfaction.
2. **Top Appreciated Features:**
   - Gasless transactions via SEP Fee-Bump Sponsorship (eliminated initial XLM friction for clean energy producers).
   - Instant digital certificate generation (PDF/JSON with QR code verification).
   - Glassmorphic real-time ESG Impact Simulator.
3. **Key User Pain Points Identified:**
   - Initial wallet connection dropdown needed clearer mobile instructions.
   - Escrow status updates needed step-by-step progress visualizer.
   - Requirement for CSV export of purchase history for corporate carbon reporting.
